THE FREE CURRICULUM

Eight units on using artificial intelligence properly, honestly and in a way that leaves you more capable. Every unit is our paraphrase of the Student Guide to Artificial Intelligence. Every exercise is marked by Claude, part by part, so you find out where your thinking is thin rather than being told well done.

Progress is kept in our database against your account, so you can stop in the middle of a unit and it will be here.
